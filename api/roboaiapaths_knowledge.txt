3. Robo Advance:
Advanced course covering robot programming, machine learning, IoT and AI technologies.

Interactive and practical project-based coding lessons to build logic and programming skills.

5. Mechatronics Course:
Students learn mechanics, electronics, robotics design and control systems.

Beginner to advanced web development course for students who want to start their developer journey.
